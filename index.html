Who we are

Half a century of
Abellana pride

We are the Graduating Class of 1975 — students who walked under the arched corridors of Abellana National School in Cebu City, forging friendships that quietly survived careers, families, migrations, and five decades of life.

Last July 2025, we came home for our Golden Jubilee — three days of reunions, laughter, and tears that reminded us why Batch 1975 was never just a class. It was a community. This portal exists so that community never loses its home.

The problem we are solving

The Golden Jubilee happened.
Where do the memories go now?

📱

Trapped in phones

Every photo and video from July 17–19 is locked inside someone's camera roll. When that phone is lost, broken, or replaced — those memories disappear with it.

💬

Buried in Viber threads

Videos shared in group chats scroll away in days. New members never see them. There is no organized, searchable, permanent archive of what happened.

✈️

Not everyone was there

Batchmates overseas, those who were ill, those who couldn't make it — they deserve to experience the Golden Jubilee too. They need a home for these memories.

∞

This portal is the answer.

One permanent, beautiful home for every photo, video, and memory from the Golden Jubilee — curated by trusted batch officers, accessible to every batchmate, preserved for the next 50 years. No platform owns it. No algorithm buries it. It is yours.

What this portal does

Three promises to
Batch 1975

01

Preserve

Every Golden Jubilee photo and video — safe, organized, and permanently hosted. No more worrying about a lost phone or a deleted chat.

02

Remember

Relive July 17–19, 2025 and the school years before it — a shared archive that grows richer with every batchmate who contributes.

03

Stay Connected

The reunion does not end when the event does. This portal keeps Batch 1975 together — for the next reunion, and for every year in between.

Q

Will my name appear publicly? Only your name and section — never your email or location unless you choose to share it.

Q

Can I submit videos? Yes — upload to Google Drive or Dropbox, set sharing to "Anyone with the link", and paste the link in the form.

Q

What if I don't have Google Drive? Send your photos via Viber directly to the portal officer who will upload on your behalf.

Q

When will my photos be published? Portal officers review weekly. You will be notified by email once your contribution goes live.

Q

Can I submit old school photos from the 1970s? Absolutely — those are the most precious. Scan or photograph them and submit the same way.

This portal is curated by designated Batch 1975 officers. Photo and video publishing is managed by 1–2 trusted administrators only.  ·  To become a contributor, email [batch-email@email.com]
